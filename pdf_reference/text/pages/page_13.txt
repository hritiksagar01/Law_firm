 
Calendar  
 
The Calendar feature in our case management software helps legal teams stay 
organized and ensures deadlines and appointments are met. Key features include: 
• 
Scheduling Court Dates  
: Easily schedule and track court hearings, 
depositions, and legal events to avoid missing critical dates. 
• 
Appointment Management  
: Schedule client meetings and internal team 
discussions with automated reminders. 
• 
Deadline Tracking  
: Monitor important case deadlines, such as filing dates 
and document submissions, to ensure timely actions. 
• 
Syncing & Integration  
: Sync your calendar across devices or systems to 
stay updated on the go. 
The Calendar feature boosts efficiency, helps manage case-related events, and reduces 
the risk of missed deadlines.
