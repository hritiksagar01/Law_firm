 
Opinion  
 
The Opinion feature in our case management software allows legal experts to provide 
insights and advice that influence case strategies and decision-making. Key aspects 
include: 
• 
Legal Advice  
: Lawyers offer professional opinions based on case facts, 
helping clients understand potential outcomes and strategies. 
• 
Case Strategy  
: Opinions help shape the approach to litigation, settlement, 
and next steps in the case. 
• 
Document Review  
: Legal teams can annotate and share their thoughts on 
case documents, ensuring alignment and clarity among stakeholders. 
The Opinion feature is essential for informed decision-making, shaping legal strategies, 
and guiding the direction of a case toward a successful outcome.
