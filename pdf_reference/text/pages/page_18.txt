 
Billing & Invoice  
 
 
The Billing & Invoice feature simplifies invoicing and payment management, ensuring 
accuracy and efficiency. Key functionalities include: 
• 
Customizable Invoices  
: Tailor invoices to your firm's billing structure, 
including services, billable hours, and expenses. 
• 
Automatic Billable Hours Tracking  
: Automatically track billable hours for 
accurate invoicing. 
• 
Expense Integration  
: Add case-related expenses directly to client invoices 
for accurate billing. 
• 
Multiple Payment Methods  
: Offer clients various payment options, such as 
credit cards, bank transfers, and online systems. 
• 
Recurring Billing  
: Set up recurring billing for retainer agreements or ongoing 
services. 
• 
Detailed Reporting  
: Generate reports to track outstanding invoices, payment 
statuses, and overall billing performance. 
• 
Payment Reminders  
: Automatically send reminders for unpaid invoices, 
ensuring timely payments. 
This feature streamlines the billing process, improves cash flow, and ensures accurate, 
timely invoicing for law firms.
