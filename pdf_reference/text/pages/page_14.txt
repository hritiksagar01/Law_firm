 
 
Events  
 
The Events feature helps track and manage important activities and milestones within a 
case or firm operations. Key features include: 
• 
Court Hearings  
: Track court hearings, trials, and legal proceedings to ensure 
no critical dates are missed. 
• 
Client Meetings  
: Schedule and manage meetings with clients, witnesses, and 
stakeholders for smooth coordination. 
• 
Deadlines  
: Monitor critical deadlines like filing dates and responses to avoid 
missed opportunities. 
• 
Internal Team Activities  
 
: Manage team meetings and internal reviews to 
keep everyone aligned. 
• 
Notifications  
: Get automatic reminders to ensure all key events are timely 
executed. 
The Events feature improves organization, ensures efficient planning, and guarantees 
the timely execution of both client-facing and internal activities.
