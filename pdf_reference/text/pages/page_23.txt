 
 
Case Report  
 
The Case Report feature offers a detailed overview of both ongoing and closed cases, 
helping legal teams stay on top of case progress, deadlines, and outcomes. Key 
functionalities include: 
• 
Case Tracking  
: Monitor the status of cases, including important milestones, 
court dates, and deadlines, offering a clear view of each case’s progress. 
• 
Task & Deadline Management  
: Keep track of tasks and deadlines to ensure 
timely completion and avoid missing crucial dates such as document filings and 
court hearings. 
• 
Case Outcome Summary  
: Generate reports summarizing the outcomes of 
closed cases, helping identify successful strategies and areas for improvement in 
future cases. 
This feature is essential for maintaining organization, tracking progress, and analyzing 
case results to optimize future case management strategies.
