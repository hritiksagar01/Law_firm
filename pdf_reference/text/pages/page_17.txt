 
Accounts  
 
The Accounts feature streamlines financial management, providing a centralized system 
for client billing, payments, and firm expenses. Key functionalities include: 
• 
Client Billing  
: Generate and track invoices based on billable hours, expenses, 
and services rendered. 
• 
Payment Tracking  
: Monitor client payments and outstanding balances to 
ensure timely collections. 
• 
Expense Logging  
: Log case-related expenses like court fees, travel, and 
administrative costs. 
• 
Account History  
: View detailed financial histories for each client, tracking 
payments, invoices, and adjustments. 
• 
Financial Reporting  
: Create customizable reports to analyze revenue, 
expenses, and profitability. 
• 
Tax & Compliance  
: Ensure compliance with tax regulations by generating 
reports for tax filing. 
This feature enhances accuracy, transparency, and efficiency in managing financial 
aspects, giving you greater control over firm finances.
