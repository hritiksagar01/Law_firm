 
Timing and Expenses  
 
 
The Timing and Expenses feature in our Case Management Software is designed to 
efficiently track billable hours, expenses, and invoicing. Key features include: 
• 
Billable Hours Tracking  
: Track time spent on cases and tasks, ensuring all 
billable minutes are captured. 
• 
Expense Management  
: Log and categorize case-related expenses like travel 
or court fees for accurate billing. 
• 
Automated Invoicing  
: Generate invoices based on tracked hours and 
expenses, reducing errors and streamlining billing. 
• 
Customizable Rates  
: Set hourly rates for team members, tasks, or case 
types to match your firm's pricing structure. 
• 
Financial Reporting  
: Get insights into billable hours, expenses, and case 
profitability for better financial planning. 
This feature simplifies billing, enhances financial accuracy, and improves resource 
management for your firm.
