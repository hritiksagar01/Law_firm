 
 
Task  
 
The Task feature in our case management software is designed to assign and track 
responsibilities within legal teams, ensuring deadlines are met and case progress is 
maintained. Key functionalities include: 
• 
Assignment  
 
: Tasks are assigned to team members based on roles and 
expertise for optimal delegation. 
• 
Deadline Management  
: Set deadlines to ensure tasks are completed on 
time, keeping cases on track. 
• 
Progress Tracking  
: Monitor task status and ensure they are completed as 
required. 
• 
Notifications  
: Automated reminders keep users updated on overdue or 
upcoming tasks. 
• 
Collaboration  
: Real-time collaboration on tasks through shared notes, 
documents, and updates. 
The Task feature promotes organization, timely completion, and effective team 
coordination for successful case management.
