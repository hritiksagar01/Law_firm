Transaction  
 
The Transaction feature enables precise tracking and management of all financial 
exchanges within your law firm. Key functionalities include: 
• 
Payment Tracking  
: Record and monitor client payments, ensuring accurate 
financial records for full and partial payments. 
• 
Expense Logging  
: Capture and associate case-related expenses, such as 
travel, court fees, and billable costs, with specific cases or clients. 
• 
Receipts & Invoices  
: Generate receipts for payments and invoices for 
services, keeping all financial documents organized. 
• 
Transaction History  
: Maintain a log of past transactions, making it easy to 
access payment, expense, and adjustment records. 
• 
Refund Management  
: Manage and document refunds or adjustments, 
ensuring client satisfaction. 
• 
Payment Methods  
: Track payments from credit cards, checks, or bank 
transfers, offering flexibility in payment options. 
• 
Real-Time Updates  
: Get instant updates on payment and expense statuses, 
ensuring current financial records. 
This feature enhances financial accuracy, transparency, and management, making it 
easier for your firm to track and process all financial transactions.
