 
Account Management: 
• 
Change Password: Securely update your password to enhance account security 
and maintain privacy. 
• 
Change Profile Picture: Personalize your account by uploading or updating your 
profile picture. 
• 
Logout: Sign out of your account to protect your data and ensure privacy when 
you’re finished. 
 
Login as a Firm  
 
The Login as a Firm feature allows firm administrators or authorized users to securely 
access the software with the following options: 
• 
User Name or Email: Enter the firm’s registered username or email address for 
login. 
• 
Password: Securely enter the password associated with the firm’s account.
