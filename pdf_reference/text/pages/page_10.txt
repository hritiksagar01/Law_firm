 
 
Clients  
 
The Clients feature enables law firms to provide a secure, transparent, and collaborative 
environment for clients. Key features include: 
• 
View Case Progress  
: Clients can track case status, milestones, and updates 
in real-time. 
• 
Secure Communication  
: A safe platform for clients to send messages, ask 
questions, and share documents with their legal team. 
• 
Review Documents  
: Clients can access, approve, or comment on case-
related documents such as contracts and legal filings. 
• 
Manage Appointments  
: Clients can track upcoming meetings, court dates, 
and important deadlines related to their case. 
This feature ensures seamless communication, transparency, and active client 
involvement throughout the legal process.
