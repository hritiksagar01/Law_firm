 
Ledger  
 
The Ledger feature helps law firms track all financial transactions accurately, ensuring 
transparency and efficiency. Key functionalities include: 
• 
Transaction History  
: Keep a comprehensive log of client payments, invoices, 
expenses, and reimbursements. 
• 
Case-Specific Ledger  
: Track financials for individual cases, ensuring 
accurate attribution of billable hours, expenses, and payments. 
• 
Categorized Entries  
: Organize transactions by categories like billable hours, 
administrative costs, and client payments. 
• 
Real-Time Updates  
: Stay updated on the status of accounts and transactions 
for better financial management. 
• 
Balance Tracking  
: Monitor outstanding balances, payments, and refunds for 
clients and firm accounts. 
• 
Audit Trail  
: Maintain a secure, transparent record of all financial activities for 
auditing or compliance purposes. 
• 
Customizable Reports  
: Generate reports to analyze cash flow, outstanding 
payments, and case-specific finances. 
This feature ensures organized financial tracking, transparency, and compliance, making 
financial management easier for law firms.
