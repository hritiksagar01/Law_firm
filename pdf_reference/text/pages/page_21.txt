 
Plan Renewal  
 
The Plan Renewal feature helps streamline and manage your firm's subscription 
renewal process. Key functionalities include: 
• 
Automatic Renewal  
: Automatically renew your subscription plan to maintain 
continuous access to all software features and services. 
• 
Renewal Notifications  
: Receive timely reminders before renewal dates to 
review and adjust your plan if needed. 
• 
Plan Upgrades  
: Easily upgrade your plan for more features, storage, or 
additional user seats as your firm's needs grow. 
• 
Payment Processing  
: Securely process renewal payments through your 
preferred payment method, simplifying the transaction. 
• 
Subscription History  
: Access detailed records of past renewals, payments, 
and changes for full transparency. 
• 
Customizable Renewal Terms  
: Choose from flexible renewal periods 
(monthly, quarterly, yearly) to fit your firm’s needs. 
This feature ensures uninterrupted software access and smooth subscription 
management, making it easier to stay up-to-date with your firm's requirements.
