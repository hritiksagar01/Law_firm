 
 
Client Activity Report  
 
The Client Activity Report tracks and logs all interactions and activities between the 
firm and its clients, ensuring transparent and effective communication. Key 
functionalities include: 
• 
Communication Log  
: Track all communications with clients, such as calls, 
emails, and messages, ensuring no important conversation is missed. 
• 
Appointment & Meeting Tracking  
: Manage and report on client meetings, 
consultations, and follow-ups, ensuring all client-facing activities are scheduled 
and documented. 
• 
Case Updates  
: Provide clients with clear, up-to-date information on case 
progress, including upcoming deadlines, hearings, and document reviews. 
This feature fosters a strong client relationship by ensuring transparency, tracking all 
activities, and keeping clients informed throughout the process.
