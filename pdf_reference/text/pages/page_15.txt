 
 
Appointment  
 
The Appointment feature helps organize and manage scheduled meetings, ensuring 
smooth coordination between legal professionals, clients, and stakeholders. Key features 
include: 
• 
Client Consultations  
: Schedule and track meetings with clients for case 
updates, legal advice, or strategy discussions. 
• 
Court Appearances  
: Organize court hearings, depositions, and trials, 
ensuring the team is fully prepared. 
• 
Internal Meetings  
 
: Plan internal team meetings, case reviews, and 
strategy sessions for alignment. 
• 
Automated Reminders  
: Receive automatic notifications for upcoming 
appointments to ensure timely preparation. 
The Appointment feature helps manage all critical meetings efficiently, avoiding 
conflicts and keeping your legal team on track.
