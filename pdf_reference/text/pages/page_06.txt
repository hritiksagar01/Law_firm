Dashboard  
 
The Dashboard provides a customizable and user-friendly interface that displays 
important information at a glance. Key features include: 
• 
Customized User Dashboard Fields: Personalize the dashboard to show 
relevant metrics, tasks, or notifications tailored to individual user preferences and 
roles. 
• 
Dashboard Items: Display key widgets such as case status, upcoming 
deadlines, recent activities, or financial summaries to keep users informed. 
• 
Client Contact & List of Matters: Easily access client contact details and view a 
list of active matters or cases, helping users stay organized and focused on their 
responsibilities. 
The Dashboard streamlines workflow by presenting critical information in one 
convenient, easy-to-navigate space.
